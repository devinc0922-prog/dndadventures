# Session recap template

Recaps go in `_sessions/` as `YYYY-MM-DD-short-title.md`, for example `_sessions/2026-10-24-the-drowned-bell.md`. They appear on the Adventure log automatically.

Keep them player-facing. No hidden plot, NPC secrets, trap locations, or monster stats: this repository and site are public.

```markdown
---
title: The Drowned Bell
session: 1
date: 2026-10-24
party: [Character One, Character Two]
summary: One or two sentences for the log list and the home page.
---

## What happened

A few paragraphs in past tense, from the party's point of view.

## People met

- **Name**: who they are and what the party knows about them.

## Loot and rewards

- Item, who took it.
- Milestone reached (if any).

## Open threads

- Things the party wants to follow up on.
```
