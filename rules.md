# House rules

<div class="prose" markdown="1">

## The game

- **D&D 5th edition**, 2014 core rules with a few 2024 rules mixed in (listed below).
- **Claude is the Dungeon Master.** The organizer shares Claude's screen on Discord and reads the narration aloud. Speak up with what your character does, and the organizer passes it along.
- **A balance of combat, exploration, and roleplay.** The table leans toward *rule of cool*: if it's fun and close to plausible, try it.

## Coming and going

The party changes from session to session, and that's expected.

- **At the start of each session**, the DM is told who's at the table, and encounters are scaled to that group.
- **If you miss a session**, your character was simply elsewhere. No explanation needed when you come back.
- **Leveling is by milestone and shared by everyone.** Missing sessions never leaves you behind.
- **Small party?** The DM may add an NPC companion to fill gaps.
- **Catch up** by reading the [adventure log]({{ '/log/' | relative_url }}).

## Rules we play differently

- **Smite spells** (Searing, Thunderous, Wrathful, and so on) use 2024 timing: cast as a bonus action right after you hit.
- **Pact of the Blade:** summoning your pact weapon is a bonus action (2024).
- **Reactions:** the DM will prompt you when a reaction such as *Shield* would change the outcome.

## Character sheets

Your sheet lives on the [Party page]({{ '/party/' | relative_url }}) and saves as you play. Only you and the DM can change your character. Everyone in the party can view it.
